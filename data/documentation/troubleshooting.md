# Troubleshooting Guide

When investigating anomalous valuation results:

1. Check recent portfolio executions and identify when behaviour changed.
2. Inspect logs for warnings as well as errors; a run may technically complete while producing a suspicious business result.
3. Correlate log evidence with known incidents and technical documentation.
4. Do not conclude a root cause solely from a generic warning. Look for matching evidence across sources.
5. Report the probable cause, supporting evidence, and a recommended next action.

A FAILED execution and an anomalous COMPLETED execution are different scenarios and should not be treated as equivalent.
